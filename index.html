We’re building Presence: a shareable introduction to you, and a reason to say hello. Share your interests, projects, work and whatever you’d like people to know. We’re exploring optional nearby discovery, with visibility always your choice.

A little context. An opening for a real conversation.

The idea, in three steps.

Your page

Choose what you share and how you present it. Use your page as a normal link, too.

Your choice

Your page and your local visibility are separate. Choose when you’re discoverable. Automatic nearby discovery is something we’re exploring—not a working feature we’re claiming today.

A reason to connect

Discover what people around you have chosen to share. Something catches your attention. What happens next is yours.

Enough to start a conversation.
