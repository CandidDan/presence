What happens on this preview?

Both interest forms are non-collecting. Trying a form does not send its values to Presence or Klaviyo, and the page does not save them in cookies or browser storage.

Vercel hosts the website and processes connection information, such as your IP address and technical request details, to serve and protect it. If an access code is enabled, an essential cookie remembers that you have entered it. See our cookie information.

Fonts and illustrations are served from this site. No analytics or Klaviyo onsite tracking script is installed. Browser autofill and a PDF download may be saved by your own browser or device according to your settings.

Proposed email signup

Once configured and approved, people could subscribe to Presence development and early-trial updates. Spaces could separately request emails about exploring a trial. The email-consent boxes start unchecked. Signing up would not create a personal or venue page, turn on nearby visibility or commit a space to a trial.

The people form would send email, optional town/city and country, and a record of the consent wording. The spaces form would send contact name, email, space name, town/city, country and any optional space type or website/social link supplied, plus the consent record. Please use a public business link rather than a private personal page.

Klaviyo would process these details to manage the selected email list and send the agreed updates. The integration does not load a tracking script and is designed to request no email-open or link-click tracking. Double opt-in is enabled for both lists, and email-open and click tracking are disabled, as confirmed by the operator. Real confirmation-email delivery has not yet been tested.

Proposed basis for email updates: your consent, which you can withdraw using the unsubscribe link in an email. Broader marketing, automated host-pack delivery and other uses are not enabled by this preview.

Keeping and deleting details

No interest-form records are collected by this preview. Before registration opens, we need to publish a retention period and a process for access, correction and deletion requests, including how unsubscribed addresses are handled to avoid sending unwanted emails again.

The retention period and responsible contact will be confirmed here before registration opens.

Providers and international processing

The current hosting provider is Vercel. The proposed email provider is Klaviyo. Their processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards must be checked against the actual account arrangements before registration opens.

Your choices

Providing optional fields would not be required to receive the relevant updates. You would be able to unsubscribe from emails at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or erase information, restrict or object to its use, and complain to your local privacy regulator.

The public privacy contact must be confirmed before we can open registration. Local discovery would need its own clear explanation and privacy choices before any such functionality is offered.
