How a trial could work in your space.

  1. 1. Plan a small trial together

    We’d agree how Presence could fit into your café, coworking space or other welcoming place, and a simple way to introduce it to visitors. Setup is still exploratory; small Bluetooth beacons are one approach we’re investigating.

  2. 2. Let visitors choose

    Visitors could share their personal page and choose separately whether to be discoverable nearby. Visibility would be off by default. The ambition is discovery without repeated check-ins, but we still need to prove the technology works.

  3. 3. Share what you learn

    You’d introduce Presence and tell us what feels useful, comfortable or unclear. Does it help people find a reason to say hello? You wouldn’t need to organise events or offer discounts.

Equipment for early trials

Any equipment used in a trial would be intended to be small and discreet, fitting quietly into your space. Our proposed approach is for Presence to supply it for early trials, rather than asking spaces to buy a beacon to test the idea. The technology and trial terms still need to be worked out; hardware requirements and detection accuracy are not confirmed.
