For spaces

A place to say hello.

A reason to say hello. In the places where people already spend time.

Presence is a customisable, shareable personal page. People would choose separately whether to be discoverable nearby, with local visibility off by default. A little context could make an in-person connection easier.

Automatic discovery without repeated venue check-ins is the ambition. The detection technology is not yet proven.

Explore a trial

Welcoming places. Voluntary connections.

Introduce the idea

Cafés, coworking spaces and other welcoming places could introduce Presence to people who spend time there. Each person would choose what to share and whether to turn nearby visibility on.

Help shape a trial

Participation would mean introducing Presence and giving feedback on a small trial: what feels useful, comfortable and clear. You wouldn’t need to organise events or offer discounts.

Explore the setup

We’re investigating small Bluetooth beacons as one possible approach. Setup is exploratory: hardware requirements, costs and detection accuracy are not confirmed.
