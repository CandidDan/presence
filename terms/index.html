What you are looking at

Presence is exploring customisable, shareable personal pages and separately optional local discoverability. The site introduces that idea; its examples are illustrative designs rather than live profiles.

Common Ground is a fictional space, not a participating venue. Photographs in the host pack illustrate settings and do not imply participation or endorsement.

Interest is not a commitment

Both forms currently send and store nothing. When registration becomes available, expressing interest would not create an account, personal page or public venue listing. A space would not be committing to a trial by asking to hear more.

Any actual trial would need agreed scope, support, privacy arrangements and a way to stop. Participation is intended to mean introducing Presence and giving feedback, rather than organising events or offering discounts.

Discovery is exploratory

The scroll illustration shows a connection idea; it is not detecting people nearby. Automatic discovery without repeated venue check-ins remains an ambition. Technology, hardware requirements, costs and accuracy are not confirmed.

Any equipment used in a trial would be intended to be small and discreet. Presence supplying early-trial equipment is a proposed approach, not a confirmed supply arrangement.

The host pack

The four-page host pack is a starter for a conversation. It explains hoped-for benefits and open questions; it does not guarantee outcomes. Licensed photographs are credited in the pack and recorded in the project’s source documentation.
