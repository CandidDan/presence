Why Presence?

Being in the same place
is a beginning.

Knowing a little about someone can give you a reason to say hello.

Presence is a customisable, shareable personal page. Someone could share a few interests or what they’re working on, then choose how to share their page. Nearby visibility would be a separate, optional choice, off by default.

01

For the people who visit

A small starting point for a real conversation, with no pressure to join in. Sharing a page would not automatically make someone discoverable nearby.

02

For your space

We hope it could help newcomers feel welcome and regulars find common ground. A shareable space page could also introduce your place and what people might connect over there.

03

For an early trial

Learn whether that context is useful in everyday visits—and whether the experience feels comfortable. Benefits to community, repeat visits or revenue are not established.

Agree the fit

Discuss your visitors, what feels appropriate, how to introduce the idea and how a trial could fit into daily routines. Timing and support would be agreed before a trial.

Give people a choice

A link or QR code could introduce Presence. Visitors would decide whether to share a page and, separately, whether to be visible nearby. Conversation stays optional.

Share what you notice

Introduce the idea and give trial feedback. You would not need to organise events or offer discounts. We’d explore whether it adds value without adding friction.

Small, discreet equipment—if needed

Small Bluetooth beacons are one approach being investigated. Any trial equipment would be intended to fit quietly into your space. Our proposed approach is for Presence to supply it for early trials rather than ask hosts to buy a beacon; supply arrangements are not yet agreed.

Automatic discovery without repeated venue check-ins is the ambition. Detection technology, hardware requirements, costs and accuracy are not confirmed. A shareable space page is also a proposed feature; this example does not create a live venue page.

A useful first conversation

What would make this
useful for your space?

We’d rather begin by listening than ask you to commit to an unfinished idea.

  • When do visitors seem open to conversation—and when do they prefer their own space?
  • What already helps newcomers feel welcome?
  • Where could a simple introduction to Presence fit naturally?
  • What would you need to know about visitor privacy, support and equipment?
  • What would make a small trial worthwhile for you?
Before anything goes live

We need an approved privacy configuration and clear arrangements for support, equipment, trial scope and how to stop. Local discovery needs technical validation. Interest would not be a commitment or a public venue listing.
