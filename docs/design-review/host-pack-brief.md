## V-1 directions — original four-page exploration

**A — practical invitation (chosen).** Lighter headline, restrained layout, practical roles in two columns, a contextual photograph after the ask. Cover motif joins two interests; page 3 annotates a smaller proposed space page.

**B — noticeboard (deliberately excessive, rejected).** Dark cover, much larger/heavier headline, stronger display contrast; host roles stack and spread across the page. Both use the same truthful proposal and voluntary participation. Rendered as a complete four-page alternate with page-fit checks. Cover and host-page previews are in `docs/host-pack/review/alternate-*.png`; source option is `--direction noticeboard`.

B differs in type, layout and mood. It was rejected because the oversized display treatment puts too much emphasis on the brand headline for a busy host's practical reading job. Pulling back to A keeps the connection metaphor as the signature and improves scan order.

## H-21 ornament log

- Cover dot/connector between the same interest: explains the conversation mechanism and builds the existing signature; not a detection demonstration.
- Short introduction dividers: distinguish two separate visitors, not a numbered process.
- Proposed space-page frame and original café artwork: show what a host might share; labelled fictional.
- Three prompt markers: reuse the single dot, connection and visibility-circle vocabulary to link the questions to welcoming, introduction and choice; they carry no exclusive information.
- Section separator before unresolved matters: separates proposed experience from unproven setup.
- One stock photograph: shows a shared-table setting relevant to the host's introduction choice; labelled as stock and not a participating venue. It is not proof of a partnership.
- No uppercase eyebrows, decorative category numbering, topic pills, heavy phone frame or coloured left-border closing are retained in the PDF.
