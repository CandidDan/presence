Cookies & storage

A small, clear footprint.

Updated 9 October 2026

The access-code cookie

If this site asks for an access code, entering it successfully sets an essential cookie named presence_gate. It remembers access for up to 30 days. It does not contain your form entries.

The cookie is limited to this site, sent over HTTPS and unavailable to page scripts. If no access code is configured, the site does not need to set it. You can remove it in your browser; a protected preview will then ask for the code again.

No analytics cookies

This preview does not install analytics, advertising or Klaviyo onsite tracking scripts. We do not use a cookie banner because no optional tracking cookies are being introduced by this version.

Interest forms remain non-collecting. A proposed Klaviyo email signup would only contact Klaviyo after you explicitly consent and submit; it would not enable onsite tracking.

Browser and device storage

The site does not put interest-form values in local or session storage. Your browser may offer autofill, cache site files or save a downloaded PDF on your device. These are controlled by your browser and device settings.
